How Stinto works

Step 1

Capture Instantly

Tap with StintoTap, share your QR code, or scan a paper business card with AI. The lead’s real contact details are in Stinto in seconds.

No typing, no lost stack of cards.

Step 2

Follow up automatically

The moment you capture a lead, Stinto sends them a personalised email with your details and the materials you chose for that event.

Step 3

Enrich with context

Record a voice note about what you discussed. Stinto turns it into text and attaches it to the lead, so the next person knows the full story.

Step 4

Sync to your CRM

One click sends the contact and your notes to HubSpot, Salesforce, Dynamics 365 or any of 9,000+ apps (via Zapier or our open API), so whoever follows up picks up exactly where you left off.

Common Questions

What happens right after I capture a lead?

Stinto instantly saves their contact details and sends them a personalised email with your details and chosen materials, with no action needed from you.

Does the automated email replace personal follow-up?

No need to choose. Stinto sends an automatic follow-up that is personalised to each lead, with your details and the materials you chose, so every lead gets a relevant first touch within minutes.

Does this work if the person I met isn't a Stinto user?

Yes. They receive your card and materials regardless of whether they use Stinto.

Is it built for trade shows and conferences?

Yes — Stinto is designed for high-volume, fast-paced meeting environments where manual follow-up is the first thing to slip.

Is it GDPR compliant?

Yes, Stinto is GDPR compliant and ISO27001 certified.

What if my new contact gives me a business card?

Stinto has a business card scanner that reads the information from the business card and saves it in Stinto. Once that has happened you can record notes and send the contact into the CRM, if you use an integration.
