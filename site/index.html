Common Questions

What is a digital business card and how does it work?

Well, it is a business card that has been digitised. But the interesting part is the benefits, which include:

  • It is easier to work with for your business relations
  • Easy to update when you change jobs
  • The ability to include more information like links and marketing material
  • You always have it on your phone 
  • Ability to share online as well as in person
 

Stinto’s digital business card can be shared with the app, through a link or by tapping the recipients’ phone with a StintoTap card. 

In Stinto’s case the other person does not need to use Stinto to exchange information with you

Do new contacts need to have a Stinto account to connect?

Nope. You can connect regardsless of whether your new contact is on Stinto or not.


When people that are not on Stinto exchange their details, they get your card in their inbox, and you get theirs in the app. If at a later point they start using Stinto, the contact can be turned into a live contact, meaning that you have updated contact  information them.
If the new contact is on Stinto already, the experience is even better.

Can I share my Stinto card online?

Yes you can. With the link option you can share it everywhere online.


From the app you can get a link to your card and use it in email signatures and the link. If you are on the mobile you also have the option to share directly through one of the apps on your phone.

Read more here.

What happens if I update my card after sharing it?

When you update your information on Stinto, the changes are effective immediately in the app. Fellow Stinto users you’ve connected with hence have your new information instantly and you have theirs. 

The changes are also made to your StintoTap card. There is no need to reactivate or take any other action.

 

Even contacts that are not using Stinto, have access to your updated information through the link they got, then you first connected.

What to do if tapping StintoTap on a phone, does not work?

You may occasionally meet a phone that cannot read the StintoTap, due to its settings or age. In these cases simply ask your new contact to scan the QR code at the back of the card and the exchange of information can start.

What if my new contact gives me a business card?

Stinto has a business card scanner that reads the information from the business card and saves it in Stinto. Once that has happened you can record notes and send the contact into the CRM, if you use an integration.

Do people need a special app to be read StintoTap?

No. All recent phones can read StintoTap cards with NFC technology. If you encounter a phone that doesn’t immediately read the StintoTap, there is a QR code you can use to connect instead.
